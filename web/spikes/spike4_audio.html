2. Key-to-sound latency

4. Background playback

Each test plays a repeating scale for 60 seconds. After starting one, switch to another tab or window for at least 20 seconds, listen for gaps or uneven timing, then come back. Test A schedules each note with an ordinary page timer; test B hands notes to FluidSynth's own sequencer three seconds ahead, timed by the audio clock.
