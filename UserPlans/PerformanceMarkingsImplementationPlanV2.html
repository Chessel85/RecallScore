3. Conflicts to resolve before building

Each carries a recommendation. Stages that depend on one say so.

  • C1. Which plan reached stage 5. Version 1 is fully committed; PI tweaks is the plan at stage 5. This plan assumes that.
  • C2. Repeats, endings and bar styles. inventory.csv gives them stave or part level, but they are score-wide today, and the barline sounds are score-wide. MusicXML writes a barline in every part, so part level would repeat “Repeat start” above every part of a quartet. Recommendation: score level when every part writes the same barline at that bar (in practice always), part level only for a part that differs.
  • C3. Note-attached fermata. The csv says Score, but it sits inside <note>, so by the new model it is Note. It also replaces the part-level row just built. A fermata on one soloist’s part would read as score-wide. Recommendation: Note in region 4 as now, plus a score-level row when every part sounding at that event carries a fermata, otherwise a part-level row.
  • C4. <direction><dynamics>. The csv gives stave, part or score, but today it becomes the dynamic attribute of the notes at that offset, and attributes are on by default. A row as well would report each dynamic twice. Recommendation: keep it a note attribute, no row. This also decides PI tweaks 7.3’s borrowed “Dynamic mf” row.
  • C5. Dashes and bracket “extend a direction”. CLAUDE.md invariant 14 says a dashed line under “cresc.” is two lines. MusicXML writes the words and the dashes start in the same <direction> element, so one length marking named by its words (“cresc. start” and “cresc. end”) reports one element, not a merge. Recommendation: adopt it, and update invariant 14’s example in CLAUDE.md.
  • C6. Unpartnered tag becomes a point. Today the parser closes any span still open at the end of a part (_flush_open_direction_spans), and region 5 says “Crescendo from bar 23, no end marked in the file”. Both change. Recommendation: adopt; the fingerprint diff is expected.
  • C7. Words as markings. Stave text is fabricated NoteData today. Turning it into marking rows means a position holding only text probably stops being a Left/Right stop. The new model makes only barline information its own event. Recommendation: let the text anchor to the next event; verify the stop behaviour first.
  • C8. Key and time signatures are listed stave, part or score. A transposing instrument writes a different key from the rest, which would make a key change part level while the cue is score-wide. Recommendation: score when all parts agree, otherwise part; low priority.
  • C9. Directives. The uncommitted stage 5 has just inverted directive defaults and the persistence key. “Parse silently” makes that throwaway. Recommendation: treat directive="yes" as system="only-top", then delete the region 1 list, its Ctrl+N, and the persistence keys.
  • C10. Barline segno and coda as barline level make them navigation stops, reversing V1 stage 7’s rows. No real score has one; only fixtures do. Recommendation: adopt.
  • C11. Two wordings for one place. PI stage 4 labels a span end “end of bar 12”; the new model gives a barline event the position “End barline”. Recommendation: region 4 position “End barline”, and “end of bar 12” wherever a bar number is needed. A location="left" barline is the end barline of the previous bar; in bar 1 or after a pickup there is none, so it reads “Start barline”.
  • C12. Region 5 as home for pedal and octave shift. Decision D15 kept them out of region 5 because a pedal-heavy piece would show a pedal row almost everywhere. Recommendation: adopt; it is what makes them toggleable with Ctrl+N.

4. The acceptance gate

Every stage touching parsers/ or models/ runs the fingerprint harnesses in tests/manual/ against a baseline from the commit before the stage (tests/manual/README.md). A green pytest run is not evidence of preserved behaviour. Each stage names the diff it expects; any other diff is a bug. After a reviewed expected diff, recapture the baseline.

Ground rules carry over from PI tweaks section 0: one stage at a time, suite green, no commit or push unless asked, no edits to wishlist.txt or the user guide, stop and report when spec and code disagree.
